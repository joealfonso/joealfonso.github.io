Employee Document Management & AI

$10M+ saved per year

Early in research I found employees using screen readers who had memorized where menu items sat, because the product couldn’t be read aloud. I led the redesign of the platform behind 4.8 billion documents in 67 countries, then designed how AI shows up across four of its product surfaces.

Amazon · A to Z app

Self-Service Resignation

Redesigning how employees quit

Resigning used to mean a form and a confirmation screen. I ran the leadership workshops, built the business case, and pushed for a flow that shows people their other options and treats the ones who still leave with respect.

Read the case study →

↳ “Growth” kept coming up in exit conversations. What people usually meant was access to training on other teams.

Discovery Education

Discovery Education Studio

Real-time collaboration for K–12 classrooms

Teachers and students were emailing, downloading and re-uploading files just to work together. I led the design of Studio: shared boards where a class can create together and see each other’s cursors move live, on whatever device they have.
